What makes it distinct

The core fantasy

Sustainability, not sprawl

You never “win” by paving the map; growth and the living island have to rise together. The goal is prosperity where the water, soil, forest and climate can actually sustain: a fresh answer to the build-forever strategy game.

Unique selling point

The land is the interface

Every choice is reflected on a living 3D island: rivers run dry, natural disasters hit, lack of forests diminishes birds and fireflies, weather and a day/night cycle roll through. The game state is represented by the landscape.

Unique selling point

A mandate, not a score screen

You don't lose on a stat tripwire. You serve three five-year terms. At the end of each, the islanders vote on your mandate, weighing four factions' trust. Consequence is political.

Unique selling point

A living web of consequence

Eight vitals, four factions and a real economy all feed each other in one connected model; only trade-offs you can see ripple across the island, follow the money trail to save coins and trace node by node on the Systems Map.

The core loop

Read the island

Eight vital systems, four factions, a real budget, all legible on the diorama and dashboard.

Act on the island

Spend a few actions each quarter: build, work the land, and fund the Research House to unlock upgrades.

Resolve the crisis

Wildfire, flood, drought and more strike at least once a year. Each is a visible trade-off, and the Elder warns you first.

Simulate the season

Watch the world advance: growth, drift, weather, consequences ripple outward.

Face the islanders' vote

Every five years the islanders vote on whether your mandate holds. Three terms, one legacy.
